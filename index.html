AI Studio — a personal video tool for one YouTube channel

Công cụ làm video cá nhân, chạy trên máy tính của chủ kênh.

AI Studio is a private desktop application that runs only on its owner's Windows PC. The owner uses it to make short educational videos and to upload them to the owner's own YouTube channel on a schedule that the owner reviews and approves. It is not sold, not distributed and has no other users.

Uses YouTube API Services. AI Studio uses the YouTube Data API v3 to upload videos that the owner created. By using AI Studio you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

What it does with YouTube

  • Signs in with Google OAuth 2.0 and asks for one permission only: youtube.upload.
  • Uploads a finished video with videos.insert (resumable upload) and, for long videos, sets the thumbnail with thumbnails.set.
  • Schedules publishing with YouTube's own publishAt time. Every video is marked as altered or synthetic content (containsSyntheticMedia), with the "made for kids" setting the owner chooses.
  • Nothing is uploaded until the owner approves the batch in the app.
  • It does not read, download, analyse or display any YouTube content, comments, analytics or other users' data.
