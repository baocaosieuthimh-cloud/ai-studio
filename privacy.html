Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 November 2026

This policy explains how AI Studio ("the app") handles information. The app is a private desktop application that runs on its owner's Windows PC and uploads videos to the owner's own YouTube channel. It has one user: the channel owner.

YouTube API Services. The app uses YouTube API Services (the YouTube Data API v3). Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. By using the app you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service.

1. Information the app accesses

When you connect YouTube, Google asks you to allow one permission: youtube.upload ("Manage your YouTube videos" — used only to upload). The app does not request your name, email address, contacts, viewing history, analytics, comments or any other YouTube or Google account data, and does not read them.

2. Information the app stores

  • OAuth tokens. The refresh token Google returns is stored only on the owner's PC, encrypted with Windows DPAPI. The short-lived access token is kept in memory only and is never written to disk.
  • Upload records. For each scheduled upload: the video ID YouTube returns, the scheduled time, the title and description the owner wrote, and the upload status. These records prevent duplicate uploads and are deleted automatically 30 days after the scheduled time.
  • Channel ID and name as returned in the upload response, used only to warn the owner if a batch would go to a different channel than the one it was approved for.

The video files themselves are the owner's own work stored on the owner's PC.

3. How the information is used and shared

The information is used only to upload and schedule the owner's videos on the owner's channel. It is sent only to Google (accounts.google.com, oauth2.googleapis.com and the YouTube Data API) over HTTPS. It is not sold, not shared with any other company or person, not used for advertising and not sent to any server run by the developer. There are no analytics or tracking services.

4. Cookies and third-party content

The app does not place cookies or similar tracking technologies on users' devices, does not show advertising and does not allow third parties to serve content or collect information through it. This website does not use cookies either.

5. How to revoke access

You can remove the app's access to your Google account at any time on the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions (choose "AI Studio dang bai", then "Remove access"). After that, the app's stored token stops working; the app detects this on its next attempt and deletes the stored token.

6. How to delete your data

All stored data lives on the owner's PC. Revoking access (above) makes the app delete its token. Upload records are deleted automatically after 30 days, and the owner can remove any record at any time in the app. To ask for deletion or for any privacy question, email baocaosieuthimh@gmail.com; requests are completed within 7 days. Videos already published on YouTube can be deleted in YouTube Studio.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children and is used only by the adult channel owner.

8. Changes

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
